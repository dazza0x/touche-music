Salon Music — Help Sheet

Starting the music (each morning)

  1. Open the Sonos app on any phone on the salon Wi-Fi.
  2. Go to Music Library → Imported Playlists.
  3. Pick a playlist (Easy Listening, R&B, Upbeat Pop…) and press Play.
  4. In the now-playing screen, check repeat is on (the loop symbol). Shuffle as you like.
  5. That's it — it plays all day. Volume is in the Sonos app too.

Music stopped?

  1. Check the Sonos app first. Is the queue just paused? Press play. If the queue is empty, start a playlist again (see above).
  2. Is the front-desk PC on? The music lives on it. Turn it on, wait two minutes, then press play in the Sonos app again.
  3. Wi-Fi trouble? If nothing on the Wi-Fi works, restart the router, wait five minutes, then press play again.
  4. Still silent? As a stop-gap, open music.touchesm.com in a browser on any phone or tablet and tap a playlist — it plays on that device, so place it somewhere audible.
  5. Then ring Dazza.
Please don't switch off or unplug the front-desk PC during opening hours — the speakers fetch the music from it. Using the PC normally (email, browsing, even watching videos) is completely fine and never interrupts the music.
